# Month-end stock count (main warehouse)

## What this is for

Counting everything in the warehouse at the end of the month so the numbers in the system
match what is really on the shelves.

## When to do it

The last working day of the month, after the last delivery truck has left and before anyone
starts picking for the next day.

## What you need first

- The stock count sheet printed for your zone.
- A pen and a phone with a calculator.
- The gate closed. No stock should move in or out while counting.

## Steps

1. Split the team by zone. One person counts, one person writes. Do not count and write on
   your own sheet at the same time.
2. Count front to back, top shelf to bottom, so nothing is skipped.
3. Count frozen items last and quickly, so the freezer door is open the shortest time.
4. Write the real number you see, even if it does not match the sheet. Do not "fix" a number
   to match.
5. Give all sheets to the shift lead. The shift lead enters the numbers into the system, not
   the counters.

## Easy mistakes to avoid

- Do not count boxes as full. Open the top box of each stack. The bottom one is sometimes
  empty or damaged.
- Items in the returns corner are NOT counted as normal stock. They have their own sheet.
- If a truck arrives during the count, park it at the gate and count it as next month.

## Who to ask

If a number is very far off (more than one full box), call the shift lead before writing it
down. Do not guess the reason yourself.

## Still checking

is not settled yet. Ask the accounting team next month. (This one point is `LOW`.)
