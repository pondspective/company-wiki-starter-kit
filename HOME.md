# Welcome to the team wiki

This is where we write down how our shop and warehouse really work, so we stop losing it
when someone is off, busy, new, or leaves.

You do not need to know computers or markdown. You just talk. Claude does the writing.

## How to start (about a minute)

1. Open this folder in Claude (see `SETUP.md` if it is not connected yet).
2. Type something like: "I want to write down how we do X."
3. Claude asks a few short questions, one at a time. Answer in your own words.
4. When you are done, Claude saves a clean draft in the `knowledge/` folder.
5. Later, you or a lead read it back with Claude to check it. Once it is right, it moves to
   `wiki/`, our trusted shelf.

That is the whole thing. If you do not know an answer, just say so. Claude writes the
question down for someone who does.

## The three shelves

- `raw/` is the messy inbox. Paste a chat, drop a photo note, or brain-dump here. Nobody has
  checked it yet.
- `knowledge/` is clean drafts, written from an interview, waiting for a second person to check.
- `wiki/` is the trusted shelf. A person checked these. Start here when you want an answer.

Ask Claude anything ("How do we count stock at month end?") and it will answer from the
trusted shelf first, and tell you if all it could find was an unchecked note.

## Good things to write down first

- A task only one person knows how to do.
- A step that is easy to get wrong.
- A "we always do it this way" rule that is not written anywhere.
- Who to call when a certain thing breaks.
