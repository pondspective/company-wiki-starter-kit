# Workaround: delivery does not match the delivery note

## The problem

A truck arrives and the box count on the delivery note does not match what is really on the
truck. It happens most with the Tuesday supplier.

## Why it happens

We do not know the real reason yet. It may be the supplier loading short, or boxes split
across two trucks. Nobody has confirmed this.

## What we actually do

1. Count the boxes at the gate before signing anything.
2. If the count is short, do NOT sign the note as it is. Write the real number you counted
   on the note, and take a photo of both the note and the load.
3. Call the shift lead before the truck leaves. Do not let it leave unsettled.

## Warnings

- Do not sign first and fix it later. That caused trouble last time.

## Open questions

- With the Tuesday supplier, the missing boxes sometimes arrive the next day. Do we hold the
  note open and add them, or close it short and start a new note? Nobody was sure. Ask
  พี่ต้อม's lead. (This part is `LOW`.)
